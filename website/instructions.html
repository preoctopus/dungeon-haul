🌐 1. First-Class Online Multiplayer

Dungeon Haul is designed ground-up for first-class online multiplayer powered by an authoritative Colyseus WebSocket server.

🔑 Private Room Codes

Create or join private 6-character room codes (e.g. DH-8842). No mandatory account registration required!

⚙️ Authoritative Server

The server simulates physics at 30 Hz. Client predicts movement while the server validates inventory, traps, and scores.

🤖 AI Seat Fill

Every game maintains exactly 4 hauler seats. Unfilled seats or disconnected players are smoothly taken over by AI controllers.

🔄 Drop-In & Reconnect

Lost your Wi-Fi? Reconnect to your active session using your session token to reclaim your seat seamlessly!

🔄 3. The Online Gameplay Loop

  1. Online Lobby & Room Code: Create or enter a private room code. Claim your class (Gnome, Sprite, Halfling, Dwarf) and toggle ready.
  2. Level 0 Treasure Hoard: The run begins in a treasure-packed vault room! Grab as many burlap sacks, crowns, and chests as you can carry.
  3. Fork Voting Chamber: At the end of each level, vote on the next biome path (e.g. Lava vs. Ice). Button-mash to argue your choice and sway the group!
  4. Dungeon Escape: Navigate dangerous biomes, hit switches, dodge spike traps, and trip opponents to steal their top stack items.
  5. End Payout & Share Titles: Upon escaping, arcane Share Modifiers are evaluated on the server. Earn titles, calculate final GP shares, and top the high-score leaderboard!

⚖️ 4. Encumbrance & Carry Stack

Haulers carry treasures in a vertical stack above their head.

  • First 3 treasures can be carried with zero movement penalty.
  • Additional items beyond 3 cause a cumulative decrease in run speed and jump height.
  • Greed Overwhelming occurs if speed drops to zero — making you an easy target for shoulder pushes!
  • If injured or stunned, your carry stack spills Sonic-style across the room!

🧮 5. Share Formula & Payout

Your final Gold Piece (GP) take is determined by the server payout formula:

Player Take = Total Dungeon GP × ( Player Shares / Sum of All Shares )

* Note: Every hauler receives a guaranteed minimum of 1 share, even if penalties would otherwise reduce their total below zero!

🌋 7. Biomes & Hazards

🏰 Dungeon

Grey brick stone walls, iron portcullis gates, retractable floor spikes, and wall torches.

🔥 Lava Biome

Basalt pillars, fiery lava pits, heat shimmers, and explosive fireball geysers.

🧊 Ice Biome

Slick slippery ice blocks, falling icicles, and frost sheen that reduces traction.

⛰️ Cavern Biome

Rough stone terrain, falling boulder shadows, crumbling ledges, and giant mushrooms.
