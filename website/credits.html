Legacy & Community

Special Thanks & TOJam 8 Heritage

Dungeon Haul originally debuted at TOJam 8 (Toronto Game Jam) under the motto "The Jam that Game Built". The game captured hearts with its chaotic 4-player couch co-op energy and unique voting chamber arguments. This modern remake preserves every ounce of that original fun while expanding it to seamless online play across modern web browsers!
