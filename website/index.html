DUNGEON HAUL

Loot the dungeon. Sabotage your friends. Race deadly traps, hoard priceless artifacts, and battle it out in the voting chamber for your fair share of the loot!

🎮 PLAY GAME DEMO 📜 HOW TO PLAY
Chaotic Multiplayer Action

Four Haulers. Endless Greed.

Grab your treasure sacks and brace yourself for fast-paced, high-stakes 2D sidescrolling platformer action designed for pure fun and slapstick competition.

👑

4-Player Heist

Race alongside three other haulers in real-time. Drop into rooms instantly via Colyseus authoritative online server synchronization or smart AI fill!

💰

Stack & Encumbrance

The more loot you carry, the heavier you get! Balance high-value treasures against your movement speed and jump height before traps catch up to you.

💥

Slapstick Sabotage

Trip rivals, shoulder-push greedy teammates into spike traps, and watch their gold spill across the room in a hilarious frantic scramble!

⚖️

Share Modifiers

The run isn't over until the loot is split! Arcane rules evaluate your gameplay — earn massive gold bonuses like Leader of the Pack or take hits for Butterfingers!
